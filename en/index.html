XIAOJIANG · A LITTLE CARE, EVERY DAY

A little care, every day.

Xiaojiang: Daily Health is an iPhone habit and reminder app. Daily records, reviews and local reminders work offline.

The app interface is currently available in Simplified Chinese only. The installed name is 小江助手; some screens retain the original name 知常 (Zhichang).

Record today

Keep track of water, movement, bedtime confirmations, meals and body measurements with your own entries.

See your days together

Review several types of records in one calendar. Trends preserve the difference between no record and zero.

Follow your own rhythm

Set water and movement reminders, a shared lunch break and weekday or rest-day routines. Use a widget and quick actions to reduce steps.
