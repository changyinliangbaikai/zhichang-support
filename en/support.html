XIAOJIANG · A LITTLE CARE, EVERY DAY

Help with your daily routine

Simple guidance for records, reminders and backups.

The app interface is currently available in Simplified Chinese only. The English App Store listing and these pages help explain the app; they do not switch its interface to English. The installed app and widget are named 小江助手. Some screens retain the original name 知常 (Zhichang). Chinese screen labels are included below so you can find the right controls.

An assistant for everyday habits

Xiaojiang helps you record and plan daily habits. It does not diagnose illness or provide treatment plans or medication-dosage advice. Consult a healthcare professional for medical decisions and follow your clinician's instructions and your own circumstances.

Records come from your confirmations

Water amounts and movement counts come from manual input, quick actions or notification confirmations. Movement reminders use your last confirmation and configured interval; they do not detect your posture. A bedtime confirmation is not a measurement of when you fell asleep or how long you slept. The initial 2,000 mL water goal is adjustable and is not a drinking prescription for everyone.

How reminders work

The app uses local iPhone notifications. Lunch breaks can pause water and movement reminders. Focus, notification summaries, permissions and system scheduling affect delivery. Opening the app or handling a reminder replenishes the notification queue; background refresh runs only when iOS permits it. Check reminder coverage on the home screen. Do not use the app as an emergency alarm or your only source of medical reminders.

Saving and restoring records

Export encrypted backups regularly as needed. Check the Files app for the actual upload status of a cloud folder. Preview a backup before restoring it. A restore replaces the current records and settings, while a separate copy of the pre-import data is retained.

If a reminder does not appear

In iPhone Settings → Notifications → 小江助手, allow notifications, then check Focus and notification summaries. Open the app and check reminder coverage on the home screen or in settings. In 设置 → 提醒与作息 (Settings → Reminders & Routine), the notification section includes 10 秒后测试提醒 (Test Reminder in 10 Seconds). Scheduling a notification successfully does not guarantee it will appear on the lock screen.

Set a shared lunch break

In 设置 → 提醒与作息 (Settings → Reminders & Routine), open the lunch-break setting in the water section or the movement section. The shared break pauses water and movement reminders. Meal, bedtime and custom-task reminders follow their own schedules.

Add the home screen widget

Open the app once first. Touch and hold an empty area of the iPhone home screen, choose Edit and Add Widget, then search for 小江助手. Touch and hold the added widget to choose what it displays. WidgetKit controls when widgets refresh.

Correct dates or add a past record

Open 记录 → 健康日历 (Records → Health Calendar), choose a date and tap the plus button to add a record, or tap an existing record to edit it. If a date conflicts with an existing entry, review the target date or choose another date. Existing data is not silently overwritten. Adding a past movement record does not restart today's movement timer.

Backups, restoration and deletion

In 设置 → 数据与备份 (Settings → Data & Backup), export an encrypted file or choose an automatic-backup folder. Keep your password safe; the developer cannot recover it. A restore requires a preview and confirmation. Deleting current records does not rewrite old backups, and external files must be removed separately. See the privacy policy for details.
