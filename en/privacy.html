XIAOJIANG · A LITTLE CARE, EVERY DAY

Privacy policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Your daily records are stored on your device. Xiaojiang has no user accounts, advertising or third-party analytics. You choose where to export or back up your records.

This is an English translation of the Simplified Chinese privacy policy included in the app. It describes the same app and data practices. The app interface is currently available in Simplified Chinese only.

Scope and contact

This policy applies to the iPhone app developed and maintained by Bond and its home screen widget. Its installed name is 小江助手 (Xiaojiang), its English App Store name is Xiaojiang: Daily Health, and its Simplified Chinese App Store name is 小江生活助手·日常健康. Its original name, 知常 (Zhichang), still appears in some screens. These names refer to the same app. Xiaojiang provides water, movement and bedtime habit reminders, records, reviews and exports. For privacy or support questions, contact stark2us@outlook.com.

Information you choose to record

You may record water amounts, movement and bedtime confirmations, meals and selected photos, sugary drinks, weight, waist measurements, and tasks, medical-instruction notes or checkup preparations that you enter yourself. The app also stores reminder times, lunch breaks, temporary routines, themes and other settings locally. These entries are used to display records, calculate summaries, schedule local notifications and export information at your request. You do not need to provide your name, phone number or identity documents, or create an account. Leaving optional records blank does not prevent use of other features. Body and health information may be sensitive personal information; enter only what you want to store on your device.

On-device storage and the widget

Daily records, settings and meal photos are stored in the app's local storage. The app and its own widget share the water, movement and action information needed for home screen displays and quick actions through the same App Group. These files are not provided to other developers' apps. Random local identifiers help manage records and prevent duplicate actions. The app does not read advertising identifiers, track you across apps or use device fingerprinting.

Notifications, photos and files

Local notifications are used only if you enable them and grant iOS permission. You can change or disable them in iPhone Settings → Notifications → 小江助手. Notification content may appear on the lock screen or be routed by the system to a paired device; you can turn off previews. You select meal photos through the system photo picker. Selected images are compressed and stored locally; the app does not request access to browse your entire photo library. Import, export and automatic backup use the system Files picker and access only locations you choose or authorize. This version does not request location, contacts, microphone, camera, HealthKit or motion-sensor permissions.

Exports, automatic backups and cloud locations

You may choose to export PDF, CSV, unencrypted JSON or encrypted backups. Anyone with access to an unencrypted file can read it, so choose where and with whom to share it. If you enable automatic encrypted backups, the app writes records to your chosen folder when it has an opportunity to run. If that folder is in iCloud Drive or another cloud service, the system or provider may upload the files and process them under its own rules. Bond does not operate or access that cloud storage. System device backups may also include app data, depending on your device and system settings. Copies in exports, cloud storage or device backups must be managed separately in those locations.

Backup passwords

A password entered for a manual export is used only for that encryption operation. Only when you enable automatic backups is the password saved in the on-device Keychain for later backups after the device has been unlocked. This Keychain item does not migrate with a device backup. Turn off automatic backups in 设置 → 数据与备份 → 自动加密备份 (Settings → Data & Backup → Automatic Encrypted Backup) to remove the stored password. Changing the password does not change passwords on existing files. Bond cannot recover or reset your encrypted-backup password.

Local diagnostics and contacting support

The app stores local state needed to schedule notifications and avoid duplicate actions. Only if you enable 提醒验证与日志 (Reminder Verification & Logs) does it record observation results, action types, reminder times, app and system versions, and other local diagnostic information. It retains up to the most recent 1,000 runtime events. Notes you enter for an observation are included in exported observation reports. Logs are not automatically uploaded to Bond. If you choose to send an email, report or screenshot, the mail provider and recipient receive the content you send. Start with a problem description and version information, and avoid unnecessary health records or identity documents. Contact information you voluntarily send is used only to reply, address your feedback and retain necessary correspondence, not for advertising or sale. After the issue is resolved, it is deleted as appropriate, except where retention is required by law.

Viewing, correcting, deleting and stopping use

You can view, correct, undo or delete entries in the daily details under 记录 (Records), the health calendar or record history. Deletion markers, local recovery copies and previously exported backups may remain to support undo and recovery. Deleting an entry does not retroactively rewrite older backups. Manage recovery copies in 数据与备份 (Data & Backup). On the automatic-backup files page, preview and confirm deletion of supported old backup files; manage other external copies through Files. Turning off reminders, canceling photo selection or disabling automatic backups does not affect other independent features. Uninstalling the app removes its local app storage but does not delete external files, cloud copies or device backups for you. Before uninstalling, we recommend turning off automatic backups to remove the stored Keychain password. There is no app account, so there is no account-deletion process. Contact the support email with privacy questions or requests concerning information you voluntarily provided to us.

Third parties and network activity

This version has no developer-operated server, advertising, third-party analytics SDK, behavior uploads or cross-app tracking. Daily reminders and records work offline. If you choose to open an external website, use email or sharing, or select a cloud file location, the relevant system or service provider may receive connection, email or file information under its own privacy rules. These voluntary actions are not described as information that never leaves your device.

Public privacy and support pages

The privacy and support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including visitors who are not signed in, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages have no health-data forms, advertising, third-party analytics scripts or health-record upload endpoint. Visiting the site does not automatically attach records from the app. Website hosting and the app's local record storage are separate data-processing contexts.

Minors

The app is intended for a general audience, is not specifically directed at children and does not ask to collect age or identity information. Minors should use it with a parent or guardian's guidance and choose goals suited to their circumstances. Do not send unnecessary identity or health information about minors to the support email.

Policy changes

If features or information-handling practices change, we will update the policy version and effective date and provide appropriate in-app notice of material changes. Any new system permission requiring authorization will be requested when you use the relevant feature. You can view the policy bundled with your app at any time in 设置 → 关于与隐私 (Settings → About & Privacy).
